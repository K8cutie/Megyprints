# Play Store listing copy

**App name (30 max):** Megy Prints: AI Photo Albums

**Short description (80 max):**
Megy designs your printed photo album for you — you just approve and order.

**Full description (4000 max):**

Making a photo album used to mean hours of dragging photos into boxes. Megy
Prints does it differently: upload your photos, and Megy — our album designer —
lays out the whole book for you. You flip through the pages, change what you
want, and order the printed album.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your album size (6×6" up to 8.5×11")
2. Upload your photos — Megy sorts them into pages by the moment they were taken
3. Review each page — reshuffle any layout you don't like with one tap
4. Order — we print, bind, and deliver

WHAT MEGY DOES FOR YOU
• Arranges photos into varied, balanced layouts — no two pages feel the same
• Matches photo shapes to frames so nothing gets awkwardly cropped
• Writes short quotes to match your album's theme (wedding, baptism, birthday,
  travel…) and drops them into the pages — swap or rewrite any of them
• Leaves room for your own words where they matter

LIVING MEMORIES (QR)
Add a QR code to a page and link it to a video or message. The code is printed
in the album — scan it years later and the memory plays. You can change what
it points to any time, even after printing.

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
Your photos stay on your device while you design. Nothing is uploaded until
you place an order — then only the finished album file goes to our print queue.

PRINTED AND DELIVERED
Choose your cover material and finish at checkout. We're a Philippine print
shop with 25 years behind the counter — real people print and check every album.

Questions? Message us through the app or our page. Walang hassle — Megy na ang
bahala sa design.
