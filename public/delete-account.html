Delete your Megy Prints account

Megy Prints (app package com.megyprints.app) is a photo-album design and printing service. You can delete your account and its data at any time, either in the app or by asking us here. There is no charge and you don’t need to reinstall anything.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Megy Prints and sign in.
  2. Go to My Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

Email megyprintsph@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We verify it’s you and delete the account within 30 days — usually the same week. We’ll confirm by email when it’s done.

Email us to delete my account

Delete some of your data — without closing your account

You don’t have to delete your whole Megy Prints account to have something removed. Email megyprintsph@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject Data removal request, and tell us what to remove — for example a saved album, an uploaded album file waiting in our print queue, a QR “living memory” link, a delivery address, or a message you sent us. We verify it’s you, remove it within 30 days (usually the same week), and confirm by email. The only records we must keep are completed-order sales records required by Philippine tax law (see below).

Email us a data removal request

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile (name, email, phone, photo).
  • Every album you saved, and the photos in them.
  • Every QR “living memory” link — any QR code already printed in an album will stop working.
  • The print-ready files we hold for your past orders, which contain your photos.
  • The delivery details on your past orders: name, phone number and address.

What we keep, and why

If you have ordered from us before, we keep a receipt-only record of that sale: the order number, the amount, the currency, the status and its dates, and the album size, material and page count. Philippine tax and accounting rules require a business to keep records of its sales, so we can’t erase them on request.

That record is stripped of everything personal — no name, no phone number, no address, no photos — and it is no longer linked to you or to any account.

If you emailed us through the contact form, that message may remain in our inbox. Say so in your deletion request and we’ll remove it too.

One case we can’t delete right away

If you have an order that is paid but not yet delivered, we still need your delivery details to finish it, so the account can’t be deleted while it’s in production. The app will tell you which order it is. Once it arrives, deletion works normally — or email us and we’ll cancel the order and delete the account together.
